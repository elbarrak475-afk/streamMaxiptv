2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to: deliver and activate your subscription, provide technical support, process payments and refunds, prevent credential sharing and fraud, and send service-related notifications (activation emails, server maintenance notices). 3. Newsletter & Marketing

Marketing emails (match schedules, promotions) are sent only with your consent. Every newsletter contains a one-click unsubscribe link, and unsubscribing never affects your active subscription.

4. Data Retention

Account records are retained for the duration of your subscription and for a limited period afterwards to satisfy accounting and anti-fraud obligations. Support correspondence is retained for 12 months. You may request earlier deletion of non-essential data at any time.

5. Third-Party Services

We rely on trusted third parties for payment processing and email delivery. These providers process data only on our instructions and under their own strict security obligations. We do not share your data with advertisers.

6. Cookies

This website uses only strictly necessary, privacy-friendly cookies (e.g., remembering your language preference). We do not use third-party advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

7. Security

All traffic is protected with 256-bit TLS encryption. Access to customer data is restricted to authorized support and billing personnel under confidentiality obligations.

8. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, or object to processing, by contacting support@getbestiptv8k.com. We respond to all verified privacy requests within 30 days.

9. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. Parental control features are available on all plans.

10. Changes to This Policy

Material changes to this Privacy Policy will be announced on this page and, where appropriate, emailed to active subscribers before taking effect.
