Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about StreamMax IPTV setup, device compatibility, free trials, and guarantees.

Our activation process is fully automated! Once payment is confirmed, your M3U link, Xtream Codes credentials (Server URL, Username, and Password), and installation instructions are delivered directly to your screen and email within 60 seconds.

You can install StreamMax on as many devices as you like. For concurrent streaming, our Starter Plan supports 1 device, Premium Plan supports 2 screens, and our Ultimate Elite Plan supports up to 4 devices streaming in 4K at the exact same time across different rooms.

We offer a 100% free 24-Hour trial with no credit card required. Simply click the "Start Free Trial" button or message our team directly via WhatsApp or Telegram. Our support agents will generate your test credentials within minutes.

No VPN is required! Our streams are securely encrypted and routed through our private CDN. However, if your local ISP throttles video streaming traffic, StreamMax is 100% compatible with all major VPNs (ExpressVPN, NordVPN, Surfshark, IPVanish, etc.).

For standard HD streams, a download speed of 15 Mbps is sufficient. For Ultra 4K and 60FPS live sports broadcasts, we recommend a stable connection of at least 30–50 Mbps. Connecting your streaming device via Ethernet or 5GHz Wi-Fi ensures optimal stability.

StreamMax is compatible with virtually every modern device: Amazon Firestick / Fire TV, Android TV boxes (Nvidia Shield, Xiaomi Mi Box), Samsung & LG Smart TVs (via IBO Player, Smart IPTV, IPTV Smarters), Apple TV, iPhone/iPad, MAG boxes, Formuler, Windows PC, macOS, and Linux.
