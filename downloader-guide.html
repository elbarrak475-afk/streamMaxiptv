Step-by-Step Setup

Install Downloader and Start Streaming

Download Guide Cover

Use this quick setup guide to prepare your device, install the app, and start watching your IPTV service in minutes.

1

Find & Install Downloader

Open the app store on your Firestick or Android device and search for Downloader. Tap Install and wait for the app to finish downloading.

2

Open Device Settings

From the home screen, go to Settings and then open My Fire TV or Device & Software, depending on your model.

3

Access Developer Options

Scroll down and select Developer Options so you can allow app installation from outside the official store.

4

Allow Unknown Sources

Find Install Unknown Apps or Apps from Unknown Sources and enable it for Downloader before you continue.

5

Launch Downloader

Open the Downloader app and click in the URL field to begin entering the installation link for your IPTV app.

6

Enter the Link or Code

Type the exact URL or code provided by your IPTV provider and select Go to begin the APK download.

7

Download the APK

Wait for the file to finish downloading. Once complete, press the download item to continue with installation.

8

Install the App

Tap Install when the system prompt appears, then allow the app to finish setup on your device.

9

Login and Start Watching

Open the IPTV app, enter your username and password, and enjoy your channels, VOD library, and live sports.
