Step-by-Step Setup

Set Up IBO Player Pro

Complete each step in order to install the app and connect your StreamMax playlist.

Step 1 image placeholder
1

Install on Smart TV or Firestick

Open the app store on your Samsung, LG, Android TV, or Firestick device. Search for IBO Player Pro, select the official app, and choose Install or Get. Launch the app when installation is complete.

Step 2 image placeholder
2

Locate Your MAC & Device Key

Open IBO Player Pro and wait for the home screen. Note the MAC address and Device Key shown on the screen. Keep both details available because they identify your device during playlist activation.

Step 3 image placeholder
3

Activate Your Playlist via the Site

Open the IBO Player activation site, enter your MAC address and Device Key, then add the M3U playlist or Xtream Codes details provided by StreamMax. Save the activation and restart IBO Player Pro to load your channels and guide.
