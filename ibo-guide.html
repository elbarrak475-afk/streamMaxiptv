Step-by-Step Setup

Set Up IBO Player Pro

Follow each step in order to install IBO Player Pro and activate IBO Player with your StreamMax playlist.

1

Install IBO Player Pro on Smart TV or Firestick

Open the app store on your Samsung, LG, Android TV, or Firestick device. Search for IBO Player Pro, select the official app, and choose Install or Get to install IBO Player Pro. Launch the app when installation is complete.

2

Locate Your MAC Address & Device Key

Open IBO Player Pro and wait for the home screen. Note the MAC address & Device Key shown on the screen. Keep both details available because they identify your device when you activate IBO Player.

3

Activate IBO Player with Your Playlist

Open the IBO Player activation site, enter your MAC address & Device Key, then add the M3U playlist or Xtream Codes details provided by StreamMax. Save the activation and restart IBO Player Pro to load your channels and guide.

4

Restart the App and Start Watching

Restart IBO Player Pro after you activate IBO Player with your playlist details. Within seconds your live channels, movies, and series will appear, and you are ready to stream StreamMax IPTV in 4K.

Now that you know how to install IBO Player Pro and activate IBO Player, choose a plan to get your service details and start streaming.
