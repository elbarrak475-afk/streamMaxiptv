Setup Instructions

Quick Start Walkthroughs

Use the steps below to connect your device, input your login details, and begin streaming in minutes.

1

IPTV Smarters Pro

  1. Download IPTV Smarters Pro from the official app store or trusted APK source on your Firestick, Android TV, or Smart TV.
  2. Open the app and select the Xtream Codes API login method.
  3. Enter the server URL, username, and password provided by StreamMax after purchase.
  4. Save the profile and wait for channels, movies, and the live TV guide to load.
2

IBO Player Pro

  1. Install IBO Player Pro from the Samsung, LG, or Fire TV app store.
  2. Launch the app and choose the option to add a playlist or Xtream connection.
  3. Paste your StreamMax M3U URL or Xtream credentials exactly as provided in your activation email.
  4. Refresh the content list and start streaming in HD or 4K with the built-in EPG.
3

XCIPTV Player

  1. Install XCIPTV on your Android box, phone, or tablet from the Play Store or a trusted APK website.
  2. Open the app and choose the login option for Xtream Codes or M3U playlist.
  3. Enter your StreamMax login, server URL, and channel list data.
  4. Allow the app to load categories, then browse sports, movies, and live TV channels.
4

TiviMate

  1. Install TiviMate on Android TV or Fire TV and launch it after the initial setup screen.
  2. Select the method to add your playlist or login credentials.
  3. Paste the Xtream or M3U details from your StreamMax account and save the profile.
  4. Open your channels list and use the guide to enjoy live content with a premium interface.
